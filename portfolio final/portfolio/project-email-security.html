Overview

Phishing emails don't always look suspicious. Many are written to look completely normal, so simple filters can let them through.

Our hackathon team built an AI-powered email security platform. I built its Threat Detection module, which combines three detection approaches into one threat probability and a final classification.

The challenge

Catch threats that are not obviously suspicious. A single check is easy to get around, so the module looks at the email from three different angles and merges what each one finds.

How it works

  1. Header analysisAn XGBoost model examines the email header and contributes its own signal to the final result.
  2. Language analysisDistilRoBERTa reads the subject and body of the email and looks for suspicious language.
  3. Rule-based checksFixed rules look for risky URLs, urgency and requests for sensitive information.
  4. Final verdictThe three signals are combined into a single threat probability and a final classification.

What I contributed

  • Built the Threat Detection module of the platform
  • Combined the header, language and rule-based approaches into one probability and final classification
  • Deployed the module as a live prototype that anyone can try

This module is one piece of a larger platform and works best alongside the other components our team built.
