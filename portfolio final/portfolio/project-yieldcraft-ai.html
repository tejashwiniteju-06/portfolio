Overview

YieldCraft AI is an AI platform that recommends crops and irrigation and fertilizer plans from soil and environmental data.

Our team delivered four machine learning modules. I helped with data preprocessing and model evaluation.

The challenge

Help with crop planning by turning soil and environmental data into crop, yield, fertilizer and irrigation guidance.

How it works

  1. Data preparationSoil and environmental data is cleaned and prepared for the models.
  2. Four ML modulesCrop recommendation, yield prediction, fertilizer recommendation and irrigation planning.
  3. ModelsClassification and regression models, including Random Forest and XGBoost.
  4. EvaluationEach model is evaluated before it is used.
