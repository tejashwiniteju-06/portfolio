Overview

Most recommenders lean on ratings and watch history. This system instead reads the scripts of movies and series to capture plot and themes, then suggests titles that are close in meaning.

I built the preprocessing and feature-extraction stage, which turns raw script text into the embeddings the rest of the system works on.

The challenge

Recommend movies and series based on what they are actually about, using the content of their scripts to produce context-aware suggestions.

How it works

  1. PreprocessingScript text is prepared for analysis.
  2. EmbeddingsSentence-BERT converts each script into an embedding that captures plot and themes.
  3. Similarity and clusteringCosine similarity and clustering group related titles together.
  4. RecommendationsContext-aware suggestions are generated from the groups and similarities.
