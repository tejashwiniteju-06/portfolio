Overview

A Flutter app that uses OpenAI's language model to analyze a resume against a job description, then generates improved content, job recommendations and interview questions.

I built it end to end, including the scoring engine and the interview-feedback extraction.

The challenge

Help job seekers tailor a resume to a specific job description and prepare for interviews in one place.

How it works

  1. AnalyzeThe OpenAI language model reviews the resume against the job description.
  2. ScoreA rule-based ATS engine uses weighted keyword matching.
  3. ImproveThe app generates better content, job recommendations and interview questions.
  4. Interview feedbackRegex extracts technical, communication and confidence scores from the AI feedback.

What I contributed

  • Built the Flutter app end to end
  • Designed the rule-based ATS scoring engine
  • Added regex-based extraction of interview scores
